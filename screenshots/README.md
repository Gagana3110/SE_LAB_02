## Notes on the burndown charts

- **Sprint 1:** the stories were created in Jira on 30 Oct, during the sprint, so the chart is flat at 0 until then. It then jumps to 34 points and burns down as stories were finished on 31 Oct. The scope-changes screenshot shows these dates.
- **Sprint 2:** points fell from 35 to 6 on 5–6 Nov. The last two stories (DFS-20 and DFS-23) were finished on 7 Nov, but their pull requests were reviewed and merged after the sprint end date, so their points drop late on the chart.
- Sprint dates were never changed after the fact. See [docs/DEVIATIONS.md](../docs/DEVIATIONS.md).
